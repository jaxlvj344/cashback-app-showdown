## **What a Cashback App Actually Does (and Why "Free Money" Isn't Magic)**

Every cashback app runs on the same engine: affiliate commissions. When you click from the app to a retailer and buy something, the retailer pays a referral fee. The app splits that fee with you. That's it. There's no secret sauce, no algorithm that "finds" money — it's a revenue-share model dressed up in a friendly interface.

The differences between apps come down to four things:

- **Coverage** — how many stores, and which categories (travel, groceries, food delivery, electronics).
- **Payout form** — real cash to PayPal/bank, gift cards, or internal "points."
- **Payout speed and floor** — how much you need to accumulate before you can withdraw, and how long it takes.
- **Stacking behavior** — whether it layers cleanly on top of credit card rewards and store loyalty programs.

A decent cashback app returns maybe 1–10% on a typical purchase. That doesn't sound life-changing, but compounded across a year of online shopping, groceries, and the occasional flight, it adds up to real money — money you were going to spend anyway.

## **How to Stack: Triple-Dipping Without Breaking Anything**

This is where cashback apps go from "nice" to "genuinely profitable." A single purchase can earn three layers of rewards if you set it up right.

**Layer 3 — Credit card rewards.** Pay with a rewards credit card that earns bonus points or cashback on the merchant's category. ShopBack doesn't care which card you use — the purchase codes as it normally would based on the merchant, so pick the card that earns the most for that spend type.

## **Common Tracking Traps (and How to Avoid Them)**

Cashback that "didn't track" is the single most common complaint about every cashback app, not just ShopBack. The causes are almost always user-side, not platform-side.

- **Switching devices mid-checkout.** Start on web, finish on mobile, and the tracking cookie is gone. Pick one and stay there.
- **Incognito or private browsing.** Tracking cookies need somewhere to live. Private mode deletes them.
- **Ad blockers left on.** Some blockers interfere with affiliate tracking. Pause them for the shopping session.
- **External coupon codes.** A code from a random coupon site can override the affiliate tracking and zero out your cashback. Use only codes listed on the merchant's ShopBack page.
- **Clicking another cashback link after activation.** If you click through Rakuten after activating ShopBack, the last click wins — and it's not yours.
- **Returning part of the order.** Returns reverse the cashback on the returned items. That's normal, not a bug.

If cashback doesn't show up in your activity within 48 hours, file a missing-cashback report through the app or support center with your order number, date, and a screenshot of the confirmation. Most platforms, ShopBack included, will investigate and credit manually if the tracking genuinely failed.
